About Us

Clear Communication, When It Matters Most

Natalia Bernal

Founder, Mastering Translations LLC

I help law firms, healthcare organizations, businesses, and individuals communicate accurately and confidently across languages.

As the founder of Mastering Translations LLC, a U.S.-based language services company, I provide professional Spanish-English interpretation and translation services for depositions, mediations, hearings, USCIS matters, medical appointments, immigration documents, academic and professional credentials, and corporate communications.

My work is grounded in linguistic precision, cultural fluency, confidentiality, and professionalism in high-stakes settings. I specialize in legal, medical, immigration, and academic documentation, including documents required for USCIS processes and for the validation of nursing and medical degrees in the United States.

In addition to my language services experience, my academic background in Political Science and International Relations, along with my experience as an educator and ontological coach, allows me to approach each assignment with strong analytical skills, cultural awareness, and a deep understanding of communication in sensitive and complex situations.

Through Mastering Translations LLC, I support clients across South Florida and nationwide with in-person and remote interpretation, simultaneous and consecutive interpretation, certified translations, and USCIS-compliant translations.

My priority is to provide accurate, reliable, and professional language support so that clients can move forward with clarity and confidence.
